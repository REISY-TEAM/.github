# REISY Code of Conduct

## Our standard

REISY contributors are expected to help create a professional, respectful, and inclusive environment.

Good conduct includes:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Giving feedback on the work, not attacking the person.
- Assuming good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Welcoming contributors with different backgrounds, skill levels, and communication styles.
- Keeping disagreements focused on facts, trade-offs, user value, security, and maintainability.

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Pressuring others to bypass review, testing, security, or privacy rules.

## Engineering discussions

Strong technical disagreement is allowed. Contributors should explain trade-offs clearly and keep the conversation connected to product quality, user safety, business risk, maintainability, and delivery.

When a discussion is stuck, ask a maintainer to summarize the decision options and choose a path.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; restrict participation; or escalate to organization owners when behavior violates this code.

Enforcement should be proportional, documented when appropriate, and focused on protecting the project and contributors.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers or organization owners. Do not use public issues for sensitive interpersonal or safety concerns.
